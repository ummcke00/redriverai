What AI Automation Does for an Ag Operation

From the feedlot to the elevator, southeast Manitoba ag businesses run on records that start on paper and end up in someone's evening. Here's what automating it looks like in practice.

🌾

Batch & Lot Traceability

Seed lots, feed batches, and treatment records captured once at the source — by form, photo, or text message — then matched, filed, and retrievable in seconds when a customer or inspector asks. No binders, no guessing.

🚜

Scale Tickets & Grain Contracts

Scale tickets read by AI and matched to contracts, settlements built automatically, and tally sheets that add themselves. What used to be a Friday-afternoon paper chase becomes a report you already have.

🧾

Invoicing & Payment Follow-Up

Invoices generated and sent the moment a load, batch, or service closes. Payment reminders chase slow payers so nobody has to make that call personally — works with QuickBooks, Wave, and Stripe.

🐄

Livestock & Compliance Records

Treatment logs, movement records, and customer paperwork flowing between your systems without re-typing. Built security-first by an 18-year cybersecurity veteran.
