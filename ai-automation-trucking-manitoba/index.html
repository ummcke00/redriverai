What AI Automation Does for a Carrier

From the Hwy 12 corridor to the Perimeter, Manitoba carriers run on paper that arrives by windshield and gets typed in by hand. Here's what automating it looks like in practice.

📄

POD & Document Matching

Proof-of-delivery photos, bills of lading, and signature slips arrive by text or email, get read by AI, matched to the right load, and filed automatically. No more driving paperwork back to the office.

🚛

Dispatch & Customer Updates

Load status emails go to your customers automatically as dispatch updates the board. Your ops team stops writing "your freight is on the way" fifty times a day.

🧾

Invoicing & Payment Follow-Up

Invoices generated and sent the moment a load closes. Payment reminders chase slow payers so your AR person doesn't have to — works with QuickBooks, Wave, and Stripe.

🛠

Maintenance & Driver Records

Work orders, service records, and driver paperwork flowing between your systems without re-typing. Built security-first by an 18-year cybersecurity veteran.
