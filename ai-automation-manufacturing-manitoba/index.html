What AI Automation Does for a Manufacturer

From custom fabricators in Winnipeg's industrial parks to cabinet shops and molders across the southeast, Manitoba manufacturers lose their margin in the paperwork between the shop floor and the office. Here's what automating it looks like in practice.

📄

Job Travelers & Work Orders

Laser cutting, welding, forming, molding, assembly — every job's traveler captured digitally and tracked through each stage automatically. On-time-delivery status stops living in a spreadsheet.

✅

ISO & QC Records

ISO 9001 quality records, inspection sheets, and per-run QA sign-offs drafted and filed automatically. Audit-ready documentation without the binder hunt — built security-first by an 18-year cybersecurity veteran.

🧾

Quotes & Invoicing

Quotes drafted from specs and your price list in hours, not days. Invoices and payment reminders generated the moment a job closes — works with QuickBooks, Wave, and Stripe.

📦

Batch Traceability & Specs

Galvanizing batches, die-cut specs, material certifications, customer order specs — captured once, flowed everywhere. Print proofs, color approvals, and revision history stop churning through email.
